Controlled replay distinguishes repair from luck

SymTrace reconstructs recorded multi-agent execution before an intervention anchor and regenerates only the downstream path, enabling more credible attribution of repair outcomes.

Validation impact: Measure failure reproduction before repair, compare targeted interventions with negative controls, and promote fixes only when they improve repeated downstream outcomes against the same captured state.
